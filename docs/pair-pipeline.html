Nutrimatic Pair Pipeline

How a sentence's word pairs are found in the two indexes, merged into one pair file, scored by phase 1, and narrowed down in seed-pairs, pfilter and pgui. Every coloured bar is a filter: a pair has to pass it to go on.

Stage by stage

1

Universe dictionary

idx/universe

Every word any later stage may use: idx/words, $BIG (words.filtered) and $DICT (dict.filtered) merged together.

rebuild.sh universe sN
  • Each word is 3 to 13 lettersfixed

Removing a word from a dictionary doesn't rebuild anything below. The dictionary check in the seed view drops those pairs instead. Adding words needs a forced rebuild.

2

Wikipedia pairs

idx/idx.2.sN

query-index lists every two-word entry in the Wikipedia phrase index that can be spelled from the sentence.

query-index $SN --wf -w2 -n0 --csv -m3 --dict idx/universe
  • Appears as a two-word entry in the Wikipedia index-w2
  • Both words are in the universe--dict
  • Each word is at least 3 letters-m3
  • Fits within the sentence's letters$SN
  • Not a NO pair (global no.pairs)--wf
3

Clue pairs

idx/cluer.sN

pairs makes every pair of clue-index words that fits the sentence. cluer then keeps the ones that appear side by side in a crossword clue. This step takes hours.

comm -12 tokens.txt idx/universe | pairs $SN -d - -m3 | cluer/query_index.py -f - -j
  • Both words are in the universe and in the clue index's vocabularycomm -12
  • Each word is at least 3 letters-m3
  • Two different words that fit within the sentence's letterspairs $SN
  • The two words are adjacent, in either order, in at least one clue-j

No NO-pair check happens here. pfilter drops NO pairs later.

4

Hand-added pairs

idx/sN.pairs

Pairs added by hand. They go into pairs.sN and are also forced into the seed view (stage 7).

No filter. Keeping their words in the dictionaries is up to whoever adds them.
5

The pair file

idx/pairs.sN

The Wikipedia, clue and hand-added pairs merged into one file per sentence.

pcomm idx/cluer.sN idx/idx.2.sN | pcomm - idx/sN.pairs | sort -u
A union, so nothing is dropped. a,b and b,a count as one pair.
6

Subset pair files (optional)

idx/pairs.sN.NAME

One per hand-made word list idx/words.sN.NAME. pgui can open one in place of pairs.sN.

rebuild.sh subsets sN
  • Both words are in words.sN.NAMEfixed
7

Seed view

not a file

The pair file filtered as it is read, using the phase 1 YES for each pair from idx/p1.idx (built by make-p1-index). Each pair is printed in the word order with the higher YES.

seed-pairs -s N [--pm .85] [-m 3] [-x 13] [-d FILE]
  • Phase 1 YES of the better word order is at least the band: .85 or .5. Pairs with no verdict are dropped.--pm · pgui seed85 / seed50
  • Each word is m to x letters (3 to 13 by default)-m -x · pgui m3/m4, x:
  • Both words are in a dictionary. Always on in pgui.-d · pgui D: DICT / BIG

Pairs from idx/sN.pairs skip the YES check, but still pass the length and dictionary checks. A forced word that isn't in the dictionary gets a warning. pgui's all source has no YES check at all.

8

pfilter

stdout, or a pgui column

The working filter for a seed: pair length, dictionary, NO pairs, and optionally letters and required words.

seed-pairs -s N | pfilter - -s N -l $SN [-u USED] [-d $DICT] [--rw FILE]
  • The pair's two words together have 9 to 15 letters-m -x
  • Both words are in the dictionary ($BIG unless -d)-d · --no-dict
  • Not a NO pair: global, plus sentence N's with -s-s N · --no-nopairs
  • Fits within the letters, less the used letters. With --exact, uses all of them.-l -u --exact
  • At least one word is a required word (idx/sN.required in pgui)--rw · pgui rw
  • Not already classified YES-y
  • "w1 w2" or "w2 w1" is in a Nutrimatic index-i

--cv doesn't filter. It sorts pairs by the consonant/vowel ratio of the letters they leave. In pgui it is on by default, and pairs outside its range are hidden.

pgui

A row of settings that every column uses, then a chain of columns. Each column takes a source, runs one command on it, and shows the result. A later column can use an earlier one as its source.

seed:idx/pairs.s1 x:13 l:letters u:used ☑BAD s:S1 … S9
seed:
The pair file the seed views read: pairs.sN or a subset. Its sN picks the forced and BAD files.
x:
Longest word allowed in any seed view.
l: u:
Letters to fit, less those already used. Checked against sentence s:'s letters.
BAD
Hides items that would leave a judged-bad letter bag (judged-bad, bad.sN).
s:
The sentence for NO pairs, required words and letter checks.
source

Where a column reads from

allseed50m3seed50m4seed85m3seed85m4

A seed view (stage 7), filtered by the column's D: dictionary, DICT or BIG. Or the dictionary itself, or an earlier column.

command

One tool per column

pfilterpairsfreqsortbag-stats

Each command uses l:, u: and s: from the settings row, plus a few options of its own.

shown

What you see

The output, minus anything BAD hides and anything outside the column's cv range. Rows can be sent to review.

pfilter

Stage 8 inside a column. It gets its dictionary from D: and its sentence from s:.

pair 9–15 letters in D: dictionary not NO for s: fits l: − u: rw ex cv sort, range 0–10

pairs

Builds word pairs (or single words) from its source that fit the letters.

fits l: − u: each word ≥ m: (3) ex: uses every letter (on) 1–2 words

freqsort

Ranks words by how much of the over-represented letters they use up.

fits l: − u: ≥ m: letters (4) cv mode, hides below 1.00

bag-stats

Ranks pairs by what the leftover letters can still make. By default it reads seed50m3, and its wb: word list is the column before it.

ex: exact (on) pair bag ≥ pb: (9) word bag ≥ wb: (5) search ss: wb,p last p counts pairs in BIG, whatever D: says keeps NO pairs
